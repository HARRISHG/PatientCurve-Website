Questions

Before you book

Is the revenue audit really free?

Yes. The audit is free, and there's no obligation to work with us afterwards. You'll get a clear view of where your clinic is missing appointments either way.

What happens after I send the form?

We'll contact you on WhatsApp or by phone to arrange a call at a time that suits you. We'll ask a few simple questions about your clinic and walk you through where we see room to grow.

Do we need to change how our clinic works?

No. PatientCurve is set up around your clinic's existing way of working, so your team can carry on as they do today — with follow-up that no longer depends on someone remembering.

Will our patients feel spammed?

No. Follow-up is friendly, relevant and spaced out sensibly. Real people step in for conversations that need a personal touch.

Is PatientCurve only for dental clinics?

Yes. PatientCurve is built specifically for dental clinics — from new patient enquiries to treatment plans and recalls.

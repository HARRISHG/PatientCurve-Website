Questions

Before you book

Is the revenue audit really free?

Yes. The audit is free, and there's no obligation to work with us afterwards. You'll get a clear view of where patients slip away in your clinic either way.

What happens after I send the form?

We'll contact you on WhatsApp or by phone to arrange a call at a time that suits you. We'll ask a few simple questions about your enquiries, visits and follow-up, and walk you through where we see room to grow.

Do we need to change how our clinic works?

No. Patientcurve runs on your clinic's own WhatsApp number and books into your Google Calendar. Your front desk marks visits and works one call list in a staff chat; the routine messages go out on their own.

What will patients be told?

Only information your clinic approves: consultation types, fees, timings and your address. We write the answers with you and you approve every word before go-live. Anything else goes to your team instead of being guessed.

Will our patients feel spammed?

No. Nothing goes out between 9 pm and 8:30 am, messages that no longer apply are cancelled, and patients can stop messages from the chat at any time.

Is Patientcurve only for dental clinics?

Yes. Patientcurve is built specifically for dental clinics, from the first enquiry to the next check-up.

1. Who we are

Patientcurve ("we", "us") provides patient journey automation for dental clinics in India: structured WhatsApp follow-up for patient enquiries, appointments and returning patients. This policy covers:

  • our website, patientcurve.com;
  • workflow demo requests and other conversations with clinics and their staff; and
  • patient data we handle on behalf of clinics that use our services (see section 4).

For questions about this policy or your data, email hello@patientcurve.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Workflow demo requests: your name, your clinic's name, your WhatsApp number, the number of clinic locations, and the main challenge you choose.
  • Conversations with us: anything you tell us on WhatsApp, by email, by phone or in a demo, such as how your clinic handles enquiries and follow-ups today.
  • Business details: if your clinic becomes a customer, contact and billing details for the people we work with.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs: our hosting provider records standard technical information when you visit the site, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time of the request. This is used to run, secure and troubleshoot the site.
  • Fonts: the site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address and browser details to Google when the page loads.

We don't use analytics tools, advertising trackers or tracking cookies on this website.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your demo request and arrange the demo, usually by contacting you on WhatsApp.
  • To understand your clinic's current process, prepare recommendations and send you a quote.
  • To provide, support and improve our services if your clinic becomes a customer.
  • To keep the website and our systems secure and working properly.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We process this data with your consent, which you give when you submit the demo form or contact us, and for other lawful purposes permitted under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You can withdraw your consent at any time (see section 9). We don't sell your personal data, and we won't add you to marketing messages you haven't asked for.

4. Patient data we handle for clinics

When a clinic uses Patientcurve, we may handle its patients' personal data to run the follow-up workflows the clinic sets up with us. This can include patient names, phone or WhatsApp numbers, the service a patient enquired about, appointment and visit status, and the messages exchanged.

  • The clinic decides. The clinic decides why and how its patients' data is used. We process it only on the clinic's instructions and under our agreement with the clinic. We don't use patient data for our own marketing or sell it.
  • Consent and opt-outs. Follow-ups go only to patients the clinic has identified as having agreed to be contacted. Patients can opt out at any time by replying to a message or telling the clinic, and opted-out patients don't receive further automated follow-ups.
  • Only what's needed. Workflows are set up to use only the information they need. We ask clinics not to send us clinical notes or other health details that aren't needed for a follow-up.
  • Patient requests. If you are a patient of a clinic that uses Patientcurve, please contact your clinic first about your data. If you contact us, we'll pass your request to the clinic and help it respond.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run Patientcurve, and only as far as they need it to do their job:

  • Website hosting and form handling (Netlify), which hosts this site and stores demo requests.
  • Messaging (WhatsApp, provided by Meta), when we or a clinic's workflow send or receive WhatsApp messages.
  • Email, scheduling and calendar tools, where they are used to arrange demos or, for clinics, connected as part of a workflow.
  • Workflow automation and reporting tools that run follow-up workflows for clinics.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts), as described in section 2.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a merger or sale of our business. In that case, this policy will continue to apply to your data.

6. Where data is stored

Some of our service providers store or process data outside India, for example in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on the provider's own security and privacy commitments, and we transfer data only as permitted under Indian law.

7. How long we keep it

  • Demo requests and conversations: for as long as we need them to respond and follow up. If we don't go on to work together, we delete them within 12 months of our last contact, or sooner if you ask.
  • Customer records: for as long as your clinic is a customer, and afterwards for as long as the law requires, for example for tax and accounting records.
  • Patient data handled for clinics: for as long as the clinic's agreement with us requires. It is deleted or returned to the clinic when the agreement ends, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • Server logs: for the period set by our hosting provider.

8. How we protect it

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal data. These include secure (HTTPS) connections, access limited to the people who need it, and reputable service providers. No method of sending or storing data is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update your data;
  • ask us to erase your data, where we no longer need it or the law doesn't require us to keep it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time — this doesn't affect anything we did before you withdrew it;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become unable to; and
  • raise a grievance with us, and, if you're not satisfied with our response, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, email hello@patientcurve.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

10. Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't set cookies or use analytics or advertising trackers. If we add any in the future, we'll update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

11. Children

Our website and demo requests are for clinic owners and staff, not children. We don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 through this website. Any patient data about children that a clinic's workflow uses is handled on that clinic's instructions, and it is the clinic's responsibility to obtain any parental consent the law requires.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or the law change. We'll change the "Last updated" date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell the clinics we work with.

13. Contact and grievances

For questions, requests or complaints about how we handle personal data, email hello@patientcurve.com. We'll acknowledge your message and respond as quickly as we can, within the time the law requires.

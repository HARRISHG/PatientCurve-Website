03 · Reminders & no-shows

Fill the chair, or free it in time.

A missed appointment costs you the slot. If the patient is never rebooked, it can cost you the whole relationship.

  • Confirm, reschedule or cancel in the chat
  • Cancellations alert your front desk straight away
  • No-shows get a rebooking message after 2 hours and 2 days, and go on the call list

Customized solution

Need something specific? We build it around your clinic.

Some clinics need more than the standard journey: a particular way of handling ad enquiries, more than one kind of consultation, or a link to the software they already use. We design a customized solution around your workflow, and every part of it is agreed with you first.

Ad enquiry handling

Every WhatsApp enquiry from your ads collects the same details in fixed steps, such as name, phone, location and requirement, so your team gets complete enquiries.

Consultation routing

Your rules decide the route. For example, local patients are offered an in-clinic consultation and patients from other towns a video consultation, each with its own information and fee.

Connections to your systems

Bookings land in your Google Calendar, and follow-up fits your practice management software. Automatic sync can be added where your software supports it.

Follow-up your way

Lists, call logging and alerts built around how your front desk already works, so nobody who enquired is forgotten.

Reports that answer your questions

For example, enquiries to appointments for each ad campaign every month, so you can see which campaigns pay for themselves.

Approved, word for word

We write every answer your patients receive, and you approve it before go-live. Anything outside it goes to your team instead of being guessed.

New enquiries are the most interested patients you'll ever have — but interest fades fast. If nobody replies quickly, or follow-up stops after one message, they book somewhere else.

  • More follow-up if they don't answer
  • An easy way to pick a time and book

  • Make it easy to schedule when they're ready

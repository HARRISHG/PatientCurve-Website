Patientcurve identifies follow-up opportunities, automates WhatsApp communication, involves your staff when needed, and tracks the outcome. It works alongside the WhatsApp, patients and clinic software you already have.

Step 1 of 4

Identify follow-up opportunities

Enquiries that haven't booked, missed appointments, unfinished treatment, inactive patients and patients due for a check-up or follow-up visit are picked up from your enquiries and appointments.

Follow-up no longer depends on someone remembering.

Step 2 of 4

Automate WhatsApp communication

Each patient gets a short, timely message from your clinic's own WhatsApp number, with an easy way to reply or book. Never at night, and never after it stops being relevant.

Every opportunity gets a consistent follow-up.

Step 3 of 4

Involve staff when needed

When a patient asks a question, wants a call or goes quiet, they land on one call list for your front desk, most urgent first.

Your team spends its time on the conversations that book.

Step 4 of 4

Track outcomes

Responses, appointments booked and attendance are tracked for every follow-up, with attributable revenue where your data permits.

You can see what follow-up is worth to your clinic.

Built to be polite

Rules every message follows.

Patients hear from their own clinic: kind, relevant, and never too often.

No messages at night

Nothing goes out between 9 pm and 8:30 am. Anything due then waits until morning.

Only answers you approve

Fees, timings and details come from information your clinic approves. Anything else goes to your team.

Out-of-date messages are cancelled

When a patient books, reschedules, cancels or opts out, messages that no longer apply are dropped.

Patients stay in control

They can stop messages from the chat at any time, and turn them back on whenever they like.

From your clinic, in your name

Messages go out from your clinic's own WhatsApp number, so patients hear from the clinic they know.

Sensible booking limits

Slots within your opening hours, with notice and a booking window you set. Your team can always book more.

Customized solution

Need something specific? We build it around your clinic.

Some clinics need more than the four standard workflows: a particular way of handling ad enquiries, more than one kind of consultation, or a link to the software they already use. We design a customized solution around your workflow, and every part of it is agreed with you first.

Ad enquiry handling

Every WhatsApp enquiry from your ads collects the same details in fixed steps, such as name, phone, location and requirement, so your team gets complete enquiries.

Consultation routing

Your rules decide the route. For example, local patients are offered an in-clinic consultation and patients from other towns a video consultation, each with its own information and fee.

Connections to your systems

Bookings land in your Google Calendar, and follow-up fits your practice management software. Automatic sync can be added where your software supports it.

Follow-up your way

Lists, call logging and alerts built around how your front desk already works, so nobody who enquired is forgotten.

Reports that answer your questions

For example, enquiries to appointments for each ad campaign every month, so you can see which campaigns pay for themselves.

Approved, word for word

We write every answer your patients receive, and you approve it before go-live. Anything outside it goes to your team instead of being guessed.

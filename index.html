The opportunity

Recover revenue from patients your clinic already has.

Your clinic already has enquiries, existing patients, WhatsApp and clinic management software. Revenue slips through the gaps between them: enquiries that never become appointments, patients who miss appointments, and patients who don't return to complete treatment or attend follow-ups.

  • They already know your clinic. They enquired, booked or visited. You don't have to win them over from scratch.
  • You've already paid to reach them. Through ads, referrals or past visits.
  • They need a timely follow-up. The right WhatsApp message at the right moment, and a person when they need one.

Before spending more to acquire new patients, recover the opportunities your clinic may already be losing.

Why clinics lose revenue

The gaps aren't in your tools. They're between them.

Your WhatsApp, your clinic software and your team each do their job. But nobody owns the follow-up in between, so it depends on busy people remembering. Each gap costs you appointments.

Enquiries go quiet

A patient asks about a treatment and doesn't book. Without a follow-up, the enquiry is forgotten in the chat.

Lost: a patient who was ready to talk

One message, then silence

Most people need more than one nudge before they book. Follow-up usually stops after the first try.

Lost: interested enquiries

Missed appointments aren't rebooked

An empty chair costs you once. A patient who never rebooks costs you every visit after.

Lost: the visit and the patient

Treatment is left unfinished

A patient starts treatment, then doesn't book the next visit. Nobody notices until months later.

Lost: treatment revenue, and the patient's care

Follow-up visits slip

Check-ups and follow-up visits depend on someone remembering to remind each patient.

Lost: steady, repeat visits

Nobody can see what's working

Without tracking replies, bookings and attendance, it's hard to know what follow-up is worth.

Lost: the case for fixing it

Customized for your clinic

Your clinic's journey, not a template.

No two clinics run the same way. Before go-live, we set up every workflow around yours, and keep adjusting it as your clinic changes.

Your answers and fees

Consultation types, fees, timings and address, in answers we write with you and you approve word for word.

Your consultation routing

Rules that send each patient to the right consultation, such as offline for local patients and video for patients from other towns.

Your booking rules

Opening hours, slot length, notice period and how far ahead patients can book, straight into your Google Calendar.

Your message timings

Follow-ups, reminders and invitations back, timed to suit your patients and your diary.

Your clinic's voice

Every message goes out in your clinic's name and tone, in English and Tamil.

Your front desk

Who gets alerts, what counts as urgent on the call list, and how calls are logged, set up around your team.

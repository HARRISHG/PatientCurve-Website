Why clinics lose revenue

Revenue rarely disappears in one big moment. It leaks out quietly.

It's not that your team doesn't care. Follow-up depends on busy people remembering, so small gaps open up between the first message and the next visit. Each one costs you appointments.

Enquiries wait too long

A patient messages about implants at 9:40 pm. By the time someone replies the next day, they've booked somewhere else.

Lost: a ready-to-book patient

Wrong or inconsistent answers

A patient told the wrong fee or timing, by a rushed reply or a generic chatbot, may never come back to check.

Lost: trust, and the booking

One message, then silence

Most people need more than one nudge before they book. Without a system, follow-up stops after the first try.

Lost: interested enquiries

No-shows aren't rebooked

An empty chair costs you once. A patient who never rebooks costs you every visit they would have made after.

Lost: the visit and the patient

Happy patients are never asked

Your team asks some patients for a Google review, on some days. Most good visits never reach your Google page.

Lost: reviews that bring new patients

Past patients drift away

Patients who haven't visited in six months rarely left unhappy. Nobody invited them back.

Lost: repeat visits

Customized for your clinic

Your clinic's journey, not a template.

No two clinics run the same way. Before go-live, we set up every part of the patient journey around yours, and keep adjusting it as your clinic changes.

Your answers and fees

Consultation types, fees, timings and address, in answers we write with you and you approve word for word.

Your consultation routing

Rules that send each patient to the right consultation, such as offline for local patients and video for patients from other towns.

Your booking rules

Opening hours, slot length, notice period and how far ahead patients can book, straight into your Google Calendar.

Your message timings

Follow-ups, reminders, rating requests and invitations back, timed to suit your patients and your diary.

Your clinic's voice

Every message goes out in your clinic's name and tone, in English and Tamil.

Your front desk

Who gets alerts, what counts as urgent on the call list, and how calls are logged, set up around your team.
